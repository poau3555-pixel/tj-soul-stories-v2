📖 GAMEPLAY GUIDE

How to Play TJ Sugar Quest

Easy to pick up, satisfying to master! Learn the candy matching secrets below.

1
🍓🍋🫐

Basic Match 3

Swap adjacent candies horizontally or vertically to line up 3 of the same fruit candy. They instantly pop, awarding score points and triggering satisfying cascades.

2
⚡

Striped Lasers (Match 4)

Match 4 candies in a straight line to create a Striped Candy. When popped in a subsequent match, it fires a beam clearing an entire horizontal row or vertical column!

3
💣

Sugar Bomb (L or T Shape)

Connect 5 candies in an intersecting "L" or "T" formation to forge a wrapped Sugar Bomb. Detonating it unleashes an explosion that wipes out a 3x3 radius!

4
🌟

Rainbow Core (Match 5 in a Line)

Line up 5 identical candies in a straight row to generate the ultimate Rainbow Core. Swap it with ANY neighbor candy to eliminate every single candy of that color from the entire board!

5
💥

Mega Special Combos

Swap two special candies directly together for unbelievable board-clearing blasts: Striped + Striped creates a Cross Blast; Striped + Bomb wipes 3 full rows and columns; Rainbow + Rainbow clears the entire board!

6
🍧🧊

Conquer Sweet Obstacles

Clear sparkling Jellies by matching candies resting directly over them. Break crunchy Frosting blockers by creating matches right beside them!

ℹ️ ABOUT THE GAME

A Sweet Journey Built for Pure Fun

TJ Sugar Quest was created to bring back the pure, joyful thrill of match-3 puzzle gaming. Guided by Pip—our cheerful candy explorer mascot—players embark on an epic quest through sugar-dusted valleys, fizzy lemon lakes, and cloud-top confectionery castles.

Unlike bloated modern mobile games filled with paywalls, countdown timers that lock you out, and intrusive ads, TJ Sugar Quest is 100% free and client-side. It runs directly inside modern web browsers with zero installation, zero trackers, and zero server wait times.

  • Zero Dependencies: Built with clean HTML5, modern CSS Grid/Flexbox, and lightweight Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TJ Sugar Quest really 100% free? +
Yes! All 15 levels, boosters, and features are completely free to play directly in your web browser. There are no paid microtransactions or subscriptions.
Will my progress and high score be saved? +
Yes! Your stars, unlocked worlds, highest score, coins, and booster inventory are automatically stored in your browser's local storage whenever you play.
How do lives regenerate? +
You start with 5 maximum lives. When you fail a level, 1 life is consumed. 1 life automatically regenerates every 15 minutes, or you can instantly refill your lives for 100 coins by tapping the heart HUD pill!
Can I play this on my iPhone, Android, or tablet? +
Absolutely. TJ Sugar Quest was built mobile-first. It responds cleanly to touch swipe gestures, tap-to-swap, and scales to fit any phone, foldable, tablet, or desktop monitor.
Can I host or deploy this game on my own website? +
Yes! Because the codebase consists of clean static HTML, CSS, and JS with zero external backend dependencies, you can deploy it to any web hosting service (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, S3, or Nginx) in seconds.
